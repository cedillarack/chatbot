# Nishitha College — Knowledge Base

## Overview
- Nishitha Degree College (NDC) / Nishitha Degree & P.G. College, Nizamabad, Telangana. Run by Nishitha Educational Academy (NEA).
- Established 1994 by Nishitha Educational Academy (registered Letter No. 5621/1993, dt. 27-12-1993). UG affiliation from Osmania University in 1994-95; now affiliated to Telangana University (college code 5022).
- Accredited by NAAC with "A" grade (awarded 2017). Placement page also refers to it as "(Autonomous)".
- ~2000+ students (UG + PG). Women and men, largely from rural Telangana.
- Founder: Late Sri. C. Bhoomaiah Garu (retired Telugu lecturer, Girraj Govt. Degree College, Nizamabad).
- Chief Patron: Sri. C. Vinaya Kumar. Chairman: Sri. C. Nikhil (MBA, Canada). Director: Dr. O.M. Shaik. Principal: Dr. K. Swapna (M.Com, MBA, Ph.D.). Co-ordinator: Sri. C. Raju.
- Exam results portal: nishithaexams.com.

## Vision & Mission
- Vision: transform students into good human beings, responsible citizens, competent professionals; focus on assimilation, creation, dissemination of knowledge; moral values, critical thinking, lifelong learning.
- Mission: quality education for profession & society; excellence in teaching-learning and research; innovation, team-spirit, entrepreneurial leadership; ethics, transparency, accountability.
- Objectives: practical training, project work, human rights/culture/environment awareness, rural student development.

## Admissions
- UG admissions via DOST (Degree Online Services, Telangana — dost.cgg.gov.in); window ~late May to late September.
- Process: DOST web options → seat allotment list → online self-reporting (pay Rs. 1000 at MeeSeva center) to confirm seat → document verification with allotment order copy.
- College reports seats filled in DOST 1st and 2nd phases every year.
- Scholarships: EPASS (telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) and National Scholarship Portal listed as links.

- Built-up area ~39,371 sq ft + 12,000 sq ft open (parking/sports). 26 classrooms, 12 tutorial rooms, seminar room, Wi-Fi campus, 16 CCTV cameras, fire safety equipment, doctor on campus, counselor (Saturdays 10 AM–1 PM), photocopy/phone, mineral water coolers, separate staff/student parking, accessibility for differently abled.
